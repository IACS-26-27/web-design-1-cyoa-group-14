Airport Adventure - Departure

Check In and Security

You step out of the taxi into the bustling airport curb. Your flight leaves in 2 hours, and you still need your boarding pass.

What do you want to do?

  • Go to the Online Check-In Portal
  • Walk over to the Terminal Kiosk

As you are checking in, a stranger in a hurry approaches you, asking you to take this random item in their hand. Before you can even process and answer, the stranger shoves the item into your hand, and flees the scene. You look around looking for a trash can, finding none. You don't think to much of it and focus on finishing checking in, trying not to be late for your flight.
